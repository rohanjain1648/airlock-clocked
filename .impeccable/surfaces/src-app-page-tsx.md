# Surface brief — Landing page (`/`)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: the four Clocked Build Challenge judges first; engineers second. Job: understand within seconds that Airlock is the agent's permission system and that all ten Clocked endpoints change outcomes. Action: run a live attack demo in the hero, then open the console (`/console`). Proof: real recorded Clocked responses for four scenarios, the 18/18 eval, the endpoint→effect map. Constraints: white ground across the product (user-pinned), animated cards (user-pinned), real icons and animated icon chips in place of every emoji (user-pinned); no invented customers, metrics or pricing.

The console (`/console`) inherits this world as an Operate extension: same tokens, icons and chips; behaviour unchanged.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is an airlock. A ticket travels through sealed chambers and a Clocked verdict decides whether each door opens. Refuses the category default of a centred headline over a row of feature cards, and the split hero with a screenshot.

OWN-WORLD: Clean-room safety signage on clinical white. Ground #ffffff, panels #f3f5f7, ink #0c1116. Signal plates in solid colour: safety red #d4283b, core amber #f2a900 (text #8a5a00), knowledge cyan #0aa5b2 (text #077682), clear green #12924f. Sealed doors carry yellow-black hazard stripes. Display lettering is Barlow Condensed, signage-derived; UI text is Geist; Geist Mono only for data, offsets and probabilities. Lucide icons inside animated icon chips.

STORY: The visitor sees a ticket stopped at a sealed door, picks an attack, watches the gates read it, believes each verdict is real (evidence quote, probability), then opens the console and runs the full board.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top bar with mark and links. Left: condensed headline at about 5.5rem, a one-line subhead, and the primary "Open the console" action. Spanning the full width beneath: the airlock strip of five chambers, with attack chips above it. On load it plays the injection case and seals door 1.

FORM: Pinned by the user (white ground, animated cards, icon chips) and carried by the airlock/clean-room signage world; the first of my ranked candidates. seed: none (direction pinned by user; roll not run).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
